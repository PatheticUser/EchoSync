Capabilities

Built for production conversations.

Every subsystem has an SLA, every metric is live, every byte is accounted for.

Zero-cloud audio

Raw microphone audio never leaves the host. VAD and transcription run locally, so ambient sound stays on your metal. Compliant by construction.

CPU edge compute

INT8-quantized CTranslate2 and ONNX graphs hold CPU load to 1.5–2.5 cores. No GPU, no dedicated instance, no cloud ingestion fees.

Streaming reasoning

Only finalized transcripts are sent upstream over TLS. Gemini Flash streams tokens back while TTS already renders the first sentence.

Overlapped synthesis

Sentence-boundary chunking masks time-to-first-audio. Kokoro synthesizes the first clause while the model is still finishing its reasoning.

Natural barge-in

Speak mid-reply and playback stops instantly. Silero VAD detects your voice and the pipeline pivots back to listening within a few frames.

Self-hosted by default

One command runs the entire stack. Docker-ready, health-probed, and instrumented for real-time observability out of the box.

Architecture

Why EchoSync sounds faster than it should.

An async, decoupled pipeline that keeps speech local and thought remote.

Local
01

Capture & wake

Audio worklet streams 512-sample PCM frames. Silero VAD gates speech with a 400 ms silence window — nothing else is ever buffered.

Local
02

Transcribe on edge

Faster-Whisper with CTranslate2 turns the spoken turn into text on the consumer CPU. Raw audio is deleted the moment it is transcribed.

Cloud
03

Reason, then speak

Only the tiny text payload travels to Gemini over TLS. Streamed tokens are chunked into sentences and rendered by Kokoro as they arrive.
